1. SYSTEM INTRODUCTION & OVERVIEW (SOFTWARE KA TA'ARUF)
================================================================================
MedPOS aik complete, fast, aur modern Medical Store, Pharmacy aur Retail Store 
Management Software hai. Is software ko fast billing counters, barcode scanners,
thermal printers (80mm aur 58mm), aur Excel spreadsheets ke sath 100% compatible 
banaya gaya hai.

Main Features:
- 1-Second Barcode Scanning & Instant POS Billing.
- 100% Genuine ISO Vector Barcode Generator & Sticker Printing Studio.
- Dual Thermal Receipt Print Support (80mm Standard & 58mm Mini Pocket Slips).
- Automatic Stock Calculation, Low Stock Alerts & Expiry Tracking.
- Customer Udhar (Credit Khata) with Printable Statements & WhatsApp Billing.
- Supplier / Distributor Ledger with Purchase Bills & Payment Logs.
- Daily Store Expense Tracker with Category-wise Breakdown.
- Automated Day Closing (Cash in Hand vs Drawer Reconciliation).
- Live Editable Audit Logs across all Sales, Purchases, Expenses, and Returns.
- Excel/CSV 1-Click Import & Export for thousands of medicine items.
- Offline-ready high-speed architecture with instant local data persistence.

3. POINT OF SALE (POS) & BILLING TERMINAL (SALE INVOICE)
================================================================================
Yeh counter ka sab se ahem hissa hai jahan par rozana billing hoti hai:

How to Create a Sale Bill (Bill Banane Ka Tareeqa):
1. USB/Bluetooth Barcode Scanner se medicine ke dibbe par mojood barcode scan karein.
   - Medicine foran bill list mein add ho jayegi aur beep sound aayegi.
2. Agar barcode nahi hai:
   - "Search Medicine" box mein medicine ka naam, generic formula, ya company 
     type karein aur Enter dabayein ya list se select karein.
3. Quantity Badhane Ka Tareeqa:
   - Barcode dubara scan karein ya table mein Quantity box par click karke number 
     change karein (+ / - buttons bhi mojood hain).
4. Customer Selection:
   - "Walk-in Customer" (Cash customer) ya dropdown se kisi purane Udhar (Credit) 
     customer ko select karein ya "+ New Customer" par click karke add karein.
5. Discount & Tax:
   - Bill par direct amount discount ya percentage (%) discount apply karein.
6. Payment Settlement (Raqam Wasool Karna):
   - "Paid Amount" mein customer se wasool shuda raqam likhein (e.g. Rs. 1000).
   - Software khud bakhud Change (Baqi wapis karne wali raqam) calculate karega.
7. Save & Print:
   - "SAVE & PRINT INVOICE" button dabayein ya keyboard se [F10] ya [Enter] dabayein.
   - Bill foran save ho kar 80mm/58mm Thermal Print preview open ho jayega.
   - Inventory stock khud bakhud kam (deduct) ho jayega.

Payment Modes Supported:
- Cash (Naqad)
- Credit / Udhar (Customer ke Khata ledger mein balance automatically chala jata hai)
- Bank / Card / EasyPaisa / JazzCash online payments.

4. THERMAL RECEIPT PRINTING GUIDE (80MM & 58MM SLIPS)
================================================================================
Software standard POS thermal receipt printers ke sath 100% calibrated hai:

1. 80mm Standard POS Slip (3-inch wide):
   - Dukan ka Name, Tagline, Address, aur Phone numbers.
   - Invoice Number, Date, Time, aur Cashier name.
   - Items table: Item Name, Quantity, Rate, aur Subtotal.
   - Total Amount, Discount, Net Payable, Received Cash, aur Change.
   - Customer Khata Balance (agar Udhar sale ho).
   - Polite footer note ("Thank You! Visit Again").

2. 58mm Mini Pocket Slip (2-inch wide):
   - Compact thermal printers ke liye specially optimized tight formatting 
     taki koi text cut na ho aur paper ki bachat ho.

Printer Settings for Perfect Printing (Chrome / Edge):
- Destination: Apna Thermal Printer select karein (e.g. Xprinter, POS-80, Black Copper).
- Paper Size: 80mm ya 58mm Roll.
- Margins: NONE (Bohot zaroori hai taki zero margin se slip print ho).
- Scale: 100% (Default).
- Headers & Footers: Uncheck (Band rakhein).

5. INVENTORY & PRODUCTS MANAGEMENT (MEDICINE STOCK RECORD)
================================================================================
Dukan ki tamam medicines aur general products ka record yahan manage hota hai:

Naya Product Add Karne Ka Tareeqa:
1. "Products" menu mein jayein aur "+ Add New Product" button dabayein.
2. Form mein darj zail maloomat darj karein:
   - Barcode SKU: Product ka barcode scan karein ya live camera scanner use karein.
   - Product Name: Medicine ka naam (e.g. Panadol Extra 500mg, Surbex Z).
   - Company / Brand: Manufacturer (e.g. GSK, Abbott, Getz Pharma).
   - Category: Pharmacy, Baby Care, Personal Care, Grocery waghera.
   - Purchase Price: Distributor se khareed ki qeemat (e.g. Rs. 15.50).
   - Retail Price: Customer ko bechne ki qeemat (e.g. Rs. 25.00).
   - Wholesale Price: Wholesale rate (Optional).
   - Current Stock Qty: Mojooda dukan ka stock (e.g. 500 packs).
   - Low Stock Alert Limit: Stock kis limit se kam ho to alert mile (e.g. 10).
3. Live Margin Calculator:
   - Software real-time mein aapko Profit Margin Percentage (%) aur Unit Profit 
     (Rs. per pack) calculate karke batata hai.
4. "Save Product" dabayein.

Editing & Deleting:
- List mein kisi bhi product ke samne Edit icon (pencil) par click karke qeemat, 
  stock, ya naam foran update karein.
- Delete icon par click karke product ko inventory se remove karein.

7. BARCODE LABEL GENERATOR & PRINTING STUDIO (STICKER PRINTING)
================================================================================
Apni dukan ke items ke liye genuine, scannable barcode stickers print karein:

1. Real Vector Barcode Engine (Powered by JsBarcode):
   - Yeh koi fake graphics nahi hain balkay 100% genuine ISO vector barcodes hain 
     jo har laser aur camera scanner par foran read hote hain.
   - Symbologies: CODE 128 (Universal), EAN-13, CODE 39, UPC-A, ITF-14, Pharmacode.

2. Three Flexible Modes:
   - Mode 1 (Single Item): Kisi aik medicine ko search karke uske 6, 12, 24 ya 
     100 stickers print karein.
   - Mode 2 (Batch Queue): Mukhtalif items ko alag alag quantities ke sath aik 
     hi roll ya sheet par print karein (Low stock items ko 1-click mein queue 
     mein la sakte hain).
   - Mode 3 (Custom / Freeform): Bina inventory ke koi bhi custom barcode, naam, 
     price, batch number, aur expiry date daal kar sticker print karein.

3. Standard Label Sizes Supported:
   - Thermal Rolls (Zebra / Xprinter / TSC / Gprinter):
     * 50mm × 30mm (2" × 1.2" Standard Pharmacy Label)
     * 50mm × 25mm (2" × 1" Compact Label)
     * 40mm × 28mm & 38mm × 25mm (Small Vials / Strips)
     * 60mm × 40mm (Syrup Bottles / Large Boxes)
     * 38mm × 25mm 2-Across (Double Column Roll)
   - A4 Sticker Sheets (Laser / Deskjet Printers):
     * A4 - 24 Labels (3×8 Grid)
     * A4 - 30 Labels (3×10 Grid)
     * A4 - 40 Labels (4×10 Grid)

4. Label Content Customization (Toggles):
   - Store Name Header (ON/OFF)
   - Product Title (ON/OFF)
   - Retail Price (ON/OFF)
   - Barcode Number (Human-readable font) (ON/OFF)
   - Brand / Company Name (ON/OFF)
   - Batch Number (B# 2026-A) (ON/OFF)
   - Expiry Date (EXP: 12/2028) (ON/OFF)

5. Live Scanner Verification Box:
   - Page ke top par test box mojood hai jahan aap apna handheld scanner point 
     karke check kar sakte hain ke sticker 100% successfully decode ho raha hai.

13. DAY CLOSING & CASH DRAWER RECONCILIATION (ROZANA HISAB KITAB)
================================================================================
Dukan band karte waqt din bhar ka mukammal hisab kitab band karein:

Day Closing Calculations:
- Opening Cash in Drawer (Subah ka jama shuda cash)
- + Total Cash Sales Wasooli (Din bhar ki naqad sales)
- + Customer Khata Recoveries (Customer udhar wasooliyan)
- - Daily Expenses Paid (Din bhar ke dukan ke kharche)
- - Supplier Cash Payments (Distributor ko kiye gaye payments)
- = EXPECTED CASH IN HAND (Dukan ke galle mein mojood hona chahiye)

Closing Entry:
- Actual Cash Counted likhein (Galle mein mojood note gin kar likhein).
- Software Cash Difference (Shortage / Excess) calculate karega.
- "PRINT DAY CLOSING AUDIT SLIP" dabayein. Owner ke liye thermal receipt print 
  ho jayegi.

17. HARDWARE SCANNERS & PRINTER SETUP TROUBLESHOOTING
================================================================================
1. Handheld Barcode Scanner Not Beeping/Reading:
   - Scanner ko USB port se connect karein. Standard USB scanner plug-and-play hota 
     hai aur keyboard wedge mode mein kaam karta hai.
   - Barcode Label Generator page par "Live Barcode Scanner Verification Tool" 
     mein scan karke check karein ke computer code receive kar raha hai.

2. Thermal Printer Margins / Text Cut Issue:
   - Print dialog mein "More Settings" par click karein.
   - Margins ko **NONE** par set karein.
   - Scale ko **100%** (Custom scale nahi) par rakhein.
   - Agar printer 58mm hai to preview mein "58mm (Mini)" button select karein.

3. Backup & Security:
   - Tamam record local secure database mein real-time persist hota hai.
   - Har maheene "Products" menu se "Export Products" aur "Reports" se Sales 
     report CSV download karke USB drive ya Google Drive par backup mehfooz rakhein.
